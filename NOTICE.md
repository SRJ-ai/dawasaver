# Data attribution

`data/drugs.json` is generated by `pipeline/build.py` from:

- **[junioralive/Indian-Medicine-Dataset](https://github.com/junioralive/Indian-Medicine-Dataset)** — MIT License.
  A curated dataset of ~254k Indian medicines (brand name, manufacturer, price,
  pack size, salt composition).

We keep only non-discontinued allopathy products, normalise composition into a
comparison key, compute a fair per-unit price, and retain the most useful
same-salt groups.

Prices and compositions are the upstream snapshot of publicly listed product
information. They may be stale or incomplete (the source carries only two salt
columns, so some multi-ingredient combinations are filtered out rather than
mis-grouped). **Always verify on the physical pack and with a pharmacist.**

DawaSaver is not affiliated with any manufacturer, pharmacy, Tata 1mg, or the
Jan Aushadhi programme.
